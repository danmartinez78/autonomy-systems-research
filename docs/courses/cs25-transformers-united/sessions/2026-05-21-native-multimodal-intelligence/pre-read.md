# Pre-Read: Native Multimodal Intelligence

**Date:** May 21, 2026
**Speaker:** Victoria Lin (Thinking Machines)
**Topic:** From Language Models to Native Multimodal Intelligence
**Slides:** [Course slide deck](https://drive.google.com/file/d/10Doblrt3Le_FpbVQoMP0DbuCIO3rtWPW/view?usp=sharing)

---

## Topic Overview

The official description frames this talk as the move from language-centric intelligence toward systems that natively integrate:

- visual information
- auditory information
- temporal structure
- real-world interaction

It also hints at two architectural tensions worth watching:

1. how much multimodal systems should inherit from strong LLM backbones
2. where modality-specific specialization becomes necessary

---

## Speaker Context

Victoria Lin works on multimodal intelligence at Thinking Machines after earlier research roles at Meta AI and Salesforce AI Research. That makes this session a useful window into how frontier labs are thinking about modality fusion after the LLM boom.

---

## Why It Matters for Autonomy

- Embodied systems do not get to stay language-only for long.
- Multimodal reasoning is core to perception, action, and situational awareness.
- The shared-versus-specialized architecture question maps directly onto robot stack design.

---

## Questions To Bring

1. What breaks first when you scale an LLM recipe into native multimodal training?
2. Which reasoning capabilities transfer cleanly from language-only models?
3. When does modality specialization outperform a single shared backbone?
4. How close are current multimodal systems to real-time interaction constraints?
